Off-Street Parking across the City of Montreal

One of Montreal's 2030 Climate Plan goals is to plant and maintain 500 000 new trees in an effort to combat urban heat islands. Part of this greening mission involves converting open-air parking lots into green areas.

This map is for borough planners, to identify potential parking area candidates as a first step in the conversion process.

What you can do here: filter between different boroughs under the City of Montreal municipality, click on any parking point to see its associated street location and hourly rate.
