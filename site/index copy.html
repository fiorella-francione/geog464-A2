Off-Street Parking across Montreal's Municipality

Montreal's 2030 Climate Plan involves a mission to plant 500 thousand new trees in an effort to adapt to climate change and combat urban heat islands. Part of this greening mission involves turning off-street parking lots into green areas.

This map is for borough planners.

What you can do here: filter between different boroughs under the City of Montreal municipality, click on any parking point to see its associated street location and hourly rate.
